<p>It&#39;s almost time for <a href="http://www.emcworld.com/index.htm" target="_self">EMC World</a> (May 4th - 7th) and I’m happy to say that once again I’ve been honored with a couple of highly coveted EMC World speaking slots!&#0160; Based on the very positive feedback I received from last year, I’ll be sticking to a similar format and focusing on:</p>
<ol>
<li>Changes in the SAN space over the past year</li>
<li>SAN related problems that are causing our customers the biggest headaches and how to avoid them</li>
<li>Up and coming SAN technologies and how they may impact your environment.</li>
</ol>
<p>Additional details about each&#0160;session are provided below.</p>
<p><strong>Title:</strong> SAN &amp; IP Storage Networking Technologies &amp; Best Practice Update</p>
<p><strong>Dates and times</strong>:&#0160;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Monday, May 4, 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM</span> and&#0160;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Wednesday, May 6, 8:30 AM - 9:30 AM</span></p>
<p><strong>Agenda</strong>:</p>
<p>What’s new with storage connectivity?</p>
<ul>
<li>Storage Trends</li>
<li>Connectrix-B (Brocade)</li>
<li>Connectrix MDS (Cisco)</li>
<li>Important concepts</li>
<li>Hot topics</li>
</ul>
<p>Storage connectivity (Protocol update)</p>
<ul>
<li>FC</li>
<li>FCoE</li>
<li>iSCSI (traditional, Lossless, iSER, RoCE, iWARP)</li>
</ul>
<p>IP SAN</p>
<ul>
<li>Dedicated versus shared</li>
<li>Deployment considerations</li>
</ul>
<p>Automation</p>
<ul>
<li>The infrastructure underlay – the missing link</li>
<li>Network automation use case – RecoverPoint for Virtual Machines and Cisco ACI</li>
</ul>
<p>&#0160;</p>
<p>I&#39;ll also be moderating the following Birds of a Feather (BoF) session. &#0160;</p>
<p><strong>Title:</strong> &quot; FC SAN: Should I stay or should I go…&quot;</p>
<p>Joining me during the BoF will be:</p>
<ul>
<li>Doug Fierro: EMC Senior Director (runs the Connectrix BU)</li>
<li>Don Pisinski: Manager, Connectrix Product Management</li>
<li>Bill Sleck: Consulting SE, SAN Health, MiTrends, Tech Refresh</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Date and time</strong>:&#0160;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Wednesday, May 6, 1:30 PM - 2:30 PM</span></p>
<p><strong>Agenda:</strong>&#0160;This is an interactive session where we ask our Customers for feedback on particular topics. &#0160;This year&#39;s BoF session abstract is:</p>
<p>Refreshing your infrastructure? &#0160;Wondering “should I converge my network now and go with an IP based Storage Area Network or should I wait<br />just a little longer to see what happens in the industry”?&#0160; Have you started the process of migrating to an IP based SAN and uncovered some of the IP SAN pitfalls?&#0160; Wondering why you can’t connect your vmknics to your NSX Logical Switches??&#0160; Thinking about how to support the connectivity needs of ScaleIO, VMware Virtual SAN or XtremIO? &#0160;If you answered yes to any of these questions, join us for a candid discussion that will be focused on storage networks and the intersections with Ethernet, IP and Network Virtualization. &#0160;We’ll provide some insight into the trends we’re seeing and you can feel free to share your thoughts and concerns or just take the opportunity to vent.</p>
<p>Hope to see you there!</p>
