<p>The updates for the month of March are listed below:</p>
<p><strong><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Brocade</span></strong>&#0160;</p>
<p><strong>Firmware added to the March ESM to support the FCoE 10-24 blade in DCX </strong>(Courtesy of Erik Paine and Aditya Nadkarni)</p>
<ul>
<li>FOS 6.4.1_FCoE – This version adds support for non-disruptive CP failover and is capable of supporting connections from both FCoE initiators and targets.</li>
</ul>

<ul>
<li>FCoE 10-24 blade</li>
</ul>
<p><strong><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Cisco MDS</span></strong></p>
<p><strong>Firmware added to the March ESM </strong>(Courtesy of Rich Hultman)</p>
<ul>
<li>Cisco NX-OS 4.2.7d</li>
</ul>
<p><strong><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Cisco UCS</span></strong> (Courtesy of Shreedhan Nikam)</p>
<p><strong>Feature support added:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>F-port trunking and channeling in NPV mode</li>
</ul>
