<p>If you’re planning to attend <a href="http://www.emcworld.com" target="_blank">EMC World 2012</a>May 21-24, The Venetian, Las Vegas, and you’re interested in FC, FCoE, iSCSI or Network Virtualization, please consider attending at least one of the following sessions:</p>
<h3>FC/FCoE – Topologies, Protocols and Limitations</h3>
<p><strong>Days and times:</strong> Tuesday 10:00a and Wed 4:15p</p>
<p><strong>Presenter:</strong> Erik Smith</p>
<p><strong>Overview: </strong>Last year, my presentation focused on answering the questions “what is FCoE” and “why/when you should use FCoE”.&#0160; This year, since the questions I&#39;m receiving have shifted away from “what is/why FCoE” and moved toward “Should I use FC or Ethernet going forward”, I decided to completely rewrite my presentation and focus on:</p>
<ul>
<li>Future proofing your infrastructure – Should&#0160;you use Physical FC or Ethernet?</li>
<li>The pros and cons of FC, FCoE and iSCSI</li>
<li>Best practices – What they are and why they matter to you</li>
<li>Network Virtualization and its eventual impact on the SAN</li>
</ul>
<h2>&#0160;</h2>
<h3>Birds-of-a-Feather: Storage Networking for the Future</h3>
<p><strong>Day and time:</strong> Tuesday 1:30p</p>
<p><strong>Presenters: </strong>Doug Fierro and Erik Smith</p>
<p><strong>Overview: </strong>The Birds-of-a-feather sessions are your chance to provide feedback on EMC’s current initiatives and help us make sure we’re focused on what matters to you.&#0160; This will be the fourth year that we’ve held a Storage Networking BoF and I can tell you from firsthand experience that we act on the information gathered during these sessions.&#0160; If you have an “Axe to grind” (related to the SAN space), come to this session and let us have it!&#0160; BTW, I’ll be providing a <a href="/brasstacks/introducing-target-driven-zoning-tdz/" target="_self">TDZ</a> update during this session.</p>
<p>&#0160;</p>
<h3>Converged Data Center: FCoE, iSCSI and the Future of Storage Networking</h3>
<p><strong>Days and times:</strong> Monday 8:30a and Wednesday 10:00a</p>
<p><strong>Presenter: </strong>David Black, Ph.D. – EMC Distinguished Engineer</p>
<p><strong>Overview: </strong>David will be providing an updated version of his extremely popular Storage Networking session.&#0160; He’ll be covering FC, FCoE, iSCSI and providing a brief overview of Network Virtualization.&#0160; If you’ve never had the pleasure of seeing David present, I would highly recommend attending this session.&#0160; As Chair of the IETF STORM Working group (iSCSI), participant in the T11 working group (FC-BB-5) that defined FCoE and as a participant in the IETF nvo3 working group (Network virtualization), you will not be disappointed with David’s level of knowledge nor his ability to clearly articulate the key points that are relevant to you.&#0160; BTW, Yes, I’m planning to attend as well.</p>
