<p>It&#39;s almost time for <a href="http://www.emcworld.com/index.htm" target="_self">EMC World</a> again (May 5th - 8th) and I&#39;m happy to say that I&#39;ve been granted the privilege of presenting to our Customers again. &#0160;I&#39;m actually very excited about this year because we&#39;re introducing a few new concepts, demonstrating at least one of them and mixing up the content of the breakout session based on the feedback we received last year. &#0160;To better explain what I mean, here&#39;s a quick overview of what we&#39;re planning.</p>
<p>To start things off, I&#39;ll be presenting:</p>
<p><strong>Title:</strong> &quot;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Storage Networking (FC/FCoE/iSCSI/VSN) Technology &amp; Best Practice Update</span>&quot;.</p>
<p><strong>Dates and times</strong>:&#0160;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Monday, May 5, 8:30 AM - 9:30 AM</span> and&#0160;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Tuesday, May 6, 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM</span></p>
<p><strong>Agenda</strong>:</p>
<ol>
<li>Protocol overview: Think of this as a &quot;Why do we care about FC again?&quot; kind of segment &#0160;</li>
<li>Product update: A brief description of product changes that have happened over the past year</li>
<li>Things you should know: A list of concepts that you should be familiar with if you deal with SANs frequently. &#0160;I&#39;ll discuss at least one of them in detail</li>
<li>Hot Topics: A list of issues that seem to be causing our customers the most pain. &#0160;Ok, it&#39;s actually a list of things that are causing our support folks the most pain, but I&#39;m expecting (hoping) that there&#39;s a bunch of overlap between these two different topics</li>
<li>IaaS and the Impact to SAN: If you&#39;ve been reading my <a href="/brasstacks/virtual-storage-networks-part-1/" target="_self">VSN blog</a> series, you&#39;ll have an idea of what I&#39;m going to talk about here.</li>
<li>OpenStack and FC: We&#39;ve done a bit of work with Brocade and others on OpenStack, so I&#39;ll briefly describe this work here.</li>
</ol>
<p>I&#39;ll also be moderating the following BoF session. &#0160;</p>
<p><strong>Title:</strong> &quot;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Storage Networking: Network Virtualization &amp; Multi-Tenant Storage (Birds-of-a-Feather)</span>&quot;</p>
<p>Joining me will be:</p>
<ul>
<li>Doug Fierro - EMC Senior Director (runs the Connectrix BU)</li>
<li>Mark Lippitt - EMC Distinguished Engineer (leads the Connectrix Advanced development team)</li>
<li>Don Pisinski - Connectrix PM (responsible for all things Connectrix-B (Brocade))</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Date and time</strong>:&#0160;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Wednesday, May 7, 1:00 PM - 2:00 PM</span></p>
<p><strong>Agenda:</strong>&#0160;This is an interactive session where we ask our Customers for feedback on particular topics. &#0160;This year&#39;s topic is:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;"><em>&quot;Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) and Network Virtualization will radically alter the way you connect to storage.&#0160; Or will it?&#0160; You tell us.&#0160; Join us for a discussion about current Storage Networking trends and the technologies that could potentially disrupt them.&quot; </em></p>
<p>We will probably also ask for feedback on:</p>
<ol>
<li>FC pain points</li>
<li>Speeds: 16, 32 and 128G FC and 10, 40 and 100G Ethernet</li>
<li>TDZ</li>
<li>OpenStack</li>
<li><a href="/brasstacks/virtual-storage-networks-part-1/" target="_self">VSN</a></li>
<li>and anything else raised by the attendees</li>
</ol>
<p>Hope to see you there!</p>
