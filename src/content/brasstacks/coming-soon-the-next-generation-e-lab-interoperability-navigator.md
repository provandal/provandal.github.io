<p>As many of you know, <a href="https://elabnavigator.emc.com/" target="_self">E-Lab Interoperability Navigator</a>&#0160;(ELN) is a free E-Lab resource that anyone can use to determine which configurations and solutions have been tested and are currently supported by EMC. &#0160;&#0160;What many of you may not fully appreciate is the staggering amount of work that it takes to keep this information relevant and up-to-date.&#0160; Consider this; 130 Engineers working in three different global locations testing over 8000 multi-vendor products.&#0160; Once tested, these new products can be used to create 20 million configurations and solutions which are then used to satisfy the 2 million queries they receive every year.&#0160;</p>
<p>A few weeks ago I heard that the ELN team was almost finished with a refresh of the user Interface and I have to say I’m really impressed.&#0160; It’s much easier to Navigate to the content that I care about, and the new solution based wizard and support envelope intersection views seem like they will be really nice to use.&#0160; Also, since I spend the majority of my time in the advanced query section, I like the fact that it has faster product selection and is more dynamic.&#0160;</p>
<p>Here’s a sneak peek, take a look for yourself!</p>

<p>If you’re an existing ELN user, you don’t need to do anything.&#0160; Starting on Monday April 14<sup>th</sup> the new site will go live and you’ll get access to the new UI automatically. &#0160;&#0160;If you are not yet a user, then check it out and see what you are missing.</p>
