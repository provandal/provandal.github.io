<p>If you’re planning to attend <a href="http://www.emcworld.com/" target="_self">EMC World 2013</a>&#0160;May 6th-9th at the Venetian, Las Vegas, and you’re interested in FC, FCoE, iSCSI or Network Virtualization, please consider attending at least one of the following sessions:</p>
<p><strong>Storage Area Networking: SAN Technology Update &amp; Best Practice Deep Dive for FC, FCoE, &amp; iSCSI SANs</strong></p>
<p>Days and times: Monday 1:00pm and Thursday 11:30am</p>
<p>Presenter: Erik Smith</p>
<p>Overview: &#0160;Based on the questions we’ve been receiving over the past few months, I decided to focus on three topics this year:</p>
<ol>
<li>An overview of what’s changed in the past 6<br />months.
</li>
<li>A technical review of the most important EMC SAN<br />best practices; and</li>
<li>The impact IaaS may have on the SAN and what you<br />can do to prepare.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>&#0160;</strong></p>
<p><strong>Birds-of-a-Feather: Storage Networking &amp; Network Virtualization</strong></p>
<p>Day and time: Wednesday 1:00pm</p>
<p>Presenters: Doug Fierro and Erik Smith</p>
<p>Overview: The Birds-of-a-feather sessions are your chance to provide feedback on EMC’s current initiatives and help us make sure we’re focused on what matters to you.&#0160; This will be the fifth year that we’ve held a Storage Networking BoF and I can tell you from firsthand experience that we act<br />on the information we gather during these sessions.&#0160; If you have an “Axe to grind” (related to the<br />SAN space), come to this session and let us have it!&#0160; BTW, yes I’ll be providing a <a href="/brasstacks/introducing-target-driven-zoning-tdz/" target="_self">TDZ</a>&#0160;demo during this session.</p>
<p>&#0160;</p>
<p><strong>Converged Data Center: FCoE, iSCSI and the Future of Storage Networking</strong></p>
<p>Days and times: Wednesday 10:00am and Thursday 8:30am</p>
<p>Presenter: David Black, Ph.D. – EMC Distinguished Engineer</p>
<p>Overview: David will be providing an updated version of his extremely popular Storage<br />Networking session.&#0160; He’ll be covering FC, FCoE, iSCSI and providing a brief overview of Network Virtualization.&#0160; If you’ve never had the pleasure of seeing David present, I would highly recommend it.&#0160;As Chair of the IETF STORM Working group (iSCSI), participant in the T11 working group (FC-BB-5) that defined FCoE and as a participant in the IETF nvo3 working group (Network virtualization), you will not be disappointed with David’s level of knowledge nor his ability to clearly articulate the key points that are relevant to you.&#0160; BTW, Yes, I’m planning to attend as well (AGAIN).</p>
Thanks for reading and I hope to see you there!
